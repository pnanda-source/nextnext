# nextnext
 
**A visual daily schedule for toddlers: what's now, what's next, and who's with me.**
 
nextnext shows a child who can't read yet how their day goes using only pictures, colors and faces. Grown-ups plan the week in a calm planner: who does drop-off, pick-up, dinner, bedtime and date night. The child's screen follows the day. It's quiet at dawn, bold by day and still at night.
 
> **Status:** designed, now being built. The design canvas is the reference, and the build is happening in [Lovable](https://lovable.dev).
 
---
 
## Why
 
Young children don't read clocks or calendars, but they care a lot about two things: **what happens next**, and **who will be there**. In families where care rotates between parents, daycare, nannies and grandparents, those handovers are where the anxiety sits. nextnext makes the handovers visible.
 
 
## Features
 
**Child's screen**
- **Now:** a large block in the caregiver's role color, with the step, a bold drawing and who's with me
- **Next:** what's coming, with its own picture, so it works without reading
- **The day in 8 squares:** open squares stand on a thick ink shadow, and done ones are pressed into the page
- **Done logic:** a step counts as done once its time passes, unless someone tapped it. A tap or an untap always beats the clock
- **The screen follows the time of day:**
  - **Sleep:** before wake-up and after bedtime, a dim moon, so it doubles as an OK-to-wake light
  - **Dawn:** soft colors, one focus, no games
  - **Day:** the full poster, with the surprises unlocked
  - **Evening:** a dark background and slow motion
- **Surprises** (day only, earned after "Get ready" is done):
  - weather, with a child dressed for it
  - a letter of the day, shown big, small and joined-up
  - a word of the day that starts with that letter
  - a daily number game
- **Grown-up gate:** press and hold for 1.2 seconds, with a ring that fills as you hold
**Grown-ups' planner**
- **Week grid, like a calendar app:** role-tinted blocks, with a "now" line and triangle
- **Detailed schedule:** shows who takes over, and where the child is right now
- **Settings-style inspector:** rows for morning, drop-off, pick-up, dinner & bath and bedtime, plus a date-night switch
- **Changes sync to the child's screen** within seconds
